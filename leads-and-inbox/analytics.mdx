---
title: "Analytics"
description: "See how your outreach is doing."
---

Click **Analytics** in the sidebar for an overview of all your outreach.

1. Choose a time range: **Last 24h**, **7-day**, **30-day**, **All time** or **Custom**.
2. Narrow it down with **All campaigns** and **All accounts**.

You see profile views, invites, accepted invites, likes, comments, messages, InMails, replies and your reply rate.

To see the numbers for a single campaign, open the campaign and click its **Analytics** tab.
