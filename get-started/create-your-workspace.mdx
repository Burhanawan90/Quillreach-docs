---
title: "Create your workspace"
description: "Sign up, verify your email and set up your workspace."
---

Your trial lasts 14 days with full access. No credit card is needed.

1. Go to [quillreach.com](https://quillreach.com) and click **Start free trial**.
2. Enter your **Name**, **Work email** and **Password**, accept the terms, and click **Create workspace**. You can also click **Continue with Google**.
3. Open the email we send you and confirm your address. If it does not arrive, click **Didn't receive it?** to send it again.
4. Answer the three short welcome steps: your name and role, your company, and your workspace name.
5. Click **Continue to Quillreach**.

You land on your **Dashboard**, where a short checklist walks you through the rest.
