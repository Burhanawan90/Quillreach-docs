---
title: "Message templates"
description: "Save messages you use often and reuse them in any campaign."
---

1. In the sidebar, click **Templates**.
2. Create a template and write your message. Use contact details such as the lead's first name to keep it personal.
3. In a campaign, open a message step and choose your template under **Template (optional)**.

Editing the step changes only that campaign. Your saved template stays the same.
