title: "Build your sequence"
description: "Choose the steps Quillreach takes with each lead."
---

A sequence is the list of steps each lead goes through. Build it on the campaign's **Builder** tab.

1. Click **Add a step**.
2. Pick from **Add an action** or **Add a condition**.
3. Fill in the step and click **Save step**.

Want a head start? Click **Use the starter sequence** and edit it.

## Actions

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Visit profile** | Views the lead's LinkedIn profile. |
| **Like post** | Reacts to the lead's recent post. You choose the **Reaction**. |
| **Comment on post** | Comments on the lead's recent post. You can **Tag the person in the comment**. |
| **Send invite** | Sends a connection request, with an optional **Connection note**. |
| **Send message** | Messages a lead you are connected to. |
| **Open Profile message** | Messages a lead with an open profile, without connecting first. |
| **Wait** | Pauses before the next step. |
| **Add tag** | Labels the lead so you can filter them later. |

## Conditions

Conditions split the sequence into two paths, for example **Is 1st connection** or **Opened message**. Other conditions are **Has LinkedIn URL** and **Is Open Profile**.

## Personalize each message

In any message step you can:

- Pick a saved **Template (optional)**.
- Click **Insert contact detail** to add the lead's name, company and more.
- Add an **AI Prompt** so the AI writes a personal line for each lead. See [AI Prompts](/ai/ai-prompts).
- Click **Preview** and pick a contact to see exactly what they will get.
