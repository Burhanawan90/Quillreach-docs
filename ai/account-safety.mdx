Keeping your LinkedIn account safe is built into Quillreach, so there is nothing for you to set up.

- **AI Pilot** drives your account and keeps it safe.
- **Smart AI Connection** keeps your account's connection human.

Your **Dashboard** shows **Smart AI Connection is on** for each account. To stop everything on an account at once, click **Stop** on its card.

## Choose how fast each account moves

1. In the sidebar, click **AI Pilot**.
2. Pick a mode for each account: **Slow**, **Normal** or **Turbo**. AI Pilot handles the rest.
3. Prefer full control? Pick **Manual** and click **Edit** to choose each daily amount yourself.

Have a question about your account? Click **Support live chat** in the sidebar.
