---
title: "Connect your LinkedIn account"
description: "Link your LinkedIn account to Quillreach in under a minute."
---

You sign in once. There is nothing to install.

1. In the sidebar, click **Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect account**.
3. Sign in to LinkedIn and complete any check LinkedIn asks for.
4. You come back to **Accounts**. Your account appears within a few seconds, marked **Connected**.
5. Click the account, then **Assign seat**, so it can send.

You can now pick this account when you create a campaign.

## If an account shows Disconnected

Open the account and click **Reconnect**, then sign in to LinkedIn again. Your campaigns pick up where they left off.

## Remove an account

Open the account and click **Disconnect** to stop using it. To remove it for good, click **Delete** and type the account name to confirm.
