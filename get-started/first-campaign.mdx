---
title: "Launch your first campaign"
description: "Set up and launch an outreach campaign in five steps."
---

<Note>You need at least one connected LinkedIn account. See [Connect your LinkedIn account](/get-started/connect-linkedin).</Note>

1. In the sidebar, click **Campaigns**, then **New campaign**.
2. Enter a **Campaign name**, choose the LinkedIn account to send from, and click **Create campaign**.
3. On the **Leads** tab, click **Add contacts** and import your leads. See [Import leads](/campaigns/import-leads).
4. On the **Builder** tab, add your steps, such as **Send invite**, **Wait** and **Send message**. Short on time? Click **Use the starter sequence**. See [Build your sequence](/campaigns/build-your-sequence).
5. Click **Run it!** to start now, or **Schedule** to pick a start time.

If something needs fixing first, Quillreach lists it under **Fix these before running the campaign**.

Follow results on the campaign's **Analytics** tab, and answer replies in your [Inbox](/leads-and-inbox/inbox).
