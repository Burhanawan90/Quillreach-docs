---
title: "Manage your leads"
description: "Find, label and organize everyone you reach."
---

Click **Leads** in the sidebar to see every lead across your campaigns.

## Find leads

- Type in **Search Leads** to find someone by name.
- Click **Filters** to narrow the list by account, campaign, label, list, company, education, skills or language. Click **Clear all** to reset.

## Work with a lead

Click a lead to open their details, or use the row menu to:

- **Edit labels**
- **Add note**
- **Message lead**
- **See profile** on LinkedIn

## Labels

Labels help you sort leads, for example "Hot" or "Follow up".

1. Open the **Labels** tab and click **New label**.
2. Enter a **Label name** and click **Create label**.
3. Add it to any lead with **Edit labels**.

You can also add labels automatically with the **Add tag** step in a campaign.

## Lists

Every import you name becomes a list on the **Lists** tab. You can rename a list or click **Delete list**.
