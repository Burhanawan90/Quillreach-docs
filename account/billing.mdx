Quillreach is one plan with every feature included.

## Pricing

- **$59** per seat per month for seats 1 to 19.
- **$39** per seat per month from the 20th seat.
- Annual billing is **$590** per seat per year, which is 2 months free.

One seat is one user with one connected LinkedIn account. See [quillreach.com/pricing](https://quillreach.com/pricing) for the full details.

## Free trial

Your trial lasts 14 days with full access and needs no credit card. The sidebar shows how many days are left. If you have not subscribed by the end of the trial, sending stops. Nothing is deleted, and you can subscribe whenever you are ready.

## Subscribe or change seats

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Choose **Monthly** or **Annual** and set your **Number of seats**.
3. Click **Subscribe**, or **Update seats** if you already have a plan.

Removing seats takes effect on your next billing date.

## Update your card or cancel

In **Settings > Billing**, click **Manage subscription**. Your past invoices are under **Settings > Invoices**.

Our refund policy is at [quillreach.com/refunds](https://quillreach.com/refunds).
