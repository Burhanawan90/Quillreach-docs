---
title: "Import leads"
description: "Add leads to a campaign from a search, a CSV file or a LinkedIn post."
---

You add leads from inside a campaign.

1. Open your campaign and go to the **Leads** tab.
2. Click **Add contacts**.
3. Pick a source:
   - **LinkedIn Search**: paste the URL of a LinkedIn people search.
   - **Sales Navigator**: paste the URL of a Sales Navigator search.
   - **Lead finder**: search for people right inside Quillreach.
   - **Import from CSV**: upload a CSV of LinkedIn profile URLs.
   - **LinkedIn Post**: import the people who commented on or liked a post.
4. Set the **Number of leads** and give the import a **List name**.
5. Click **Start import**.

Leads appear in the campaign as they are added. Anyone already in the campaign is skipped. Each import is also saved as a list under **Leads > Lists**, so you can reuse it.

<Tip>For a CSV file, put the LinkedIn profile URL in the first column.</Tip>

When you are done, click **Next: build your sequence**.
