---
title: "Inbox"
description: "Read and answer every LinkedIn conversation in one place."
---

**Inbox** brings the conversations from all your connected LinkedIn accounts together.

## Find a conversation

- Type in **Search conversations**.
- Use the quick filters **Favorite**, **Unread** and **Unreplied**.
- Click **Filters** to show only certain accounts or labels, then **Apply filters**.

## Reply

1. Open a conversation.
2. Type in **Write your message here**. Click **Insert contact detail** to add the lead's name or company.
3. Click **Send**.

You can edit or delete a message you sent.

## More options

From a conversation's menu you can **View profile**, **Mark as unread** or **Export conversation**. To keep someone you are chatting with, click **Add to leads**.

<Note>Teammates with read-only access can read conversations but not reply.</Note>
