---

An AI Prompt is a reusable instruction the AI follows to personalize part of a message for each lead.

## Use a prompt in a campaign

1. Open a message, invite or comment step in your campaign's **Builder**.
2. Choose an **AI Prompt**.
3. Click **Preview** and pick a contact to see the message the AI writes for them.
4. Click **Save step**.

## Create your own prompt

1. In the sidebar, click **AI Prompts**.
2. Start from one on the **Prebuilt** tab and click **Duplicate**, or click **Create my prompt**.
3. Write what you want the AI to do, for example "Mention something from their recent post".
4. Save it. It now appears under **My Prompts** and in every campaign.

<Note>AI-written messages wait for your approval by default. Nothing sends until you approve or edit it.</Note>
