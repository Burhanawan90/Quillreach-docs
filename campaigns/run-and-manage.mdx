---
title: "Run, schedule and pause campaigns"
description: "Start a campaign, set its hours and pause it at any time."
---

## Start or schedule

- Click **Run it!** to start now.
- Click **Schedule** to pick a start date and time. To change your mind, click **Cancel schedule**.

## Set when it runs

On the campaign's **Settings** tab, open **Schedule** and click **Add a schedule**. Pick the days, hours and timezone, then click **Save schedule**.

## Pause and resume

Click **Pause** on the campaign, or use the toggle in the **Campaigns** list. Click **Resume** to carry on from where it stopped.

## Other campaign settings

On the **Settings** tab you can also:

- Rename the campaign.
- Turn on **Lead filtering** to skip leads you do not want.
- Turn on **Auto-withdraw pending invites** to clear invites nobody accepted.
- Click **Delete campaign**.

## Campaign status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Still being set up. |
| **Ready** | Set up and waiting for you to run it. |
| **Scheduled** | Will start at the time you picked. |
| **Active** | Running now. |
| **Paused** | Stopped until you click **Resume**. |
| **Finished** | Every lead has been through the sequence. |

To reuse a campaign, click **Duplicate** in the row menu on the **Campaigns** list.
