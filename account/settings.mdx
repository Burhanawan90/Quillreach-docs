title: "Profile and security"
description: "Update your details, notifications and sign-ins."
---

## Profile

Go to **Settings > Profile settings** to change your name, email or password, and choose which updates you get by email, SMS or web push.

## Security

Go to **Settings > Security** to see your recent sign-ins. If you spot one you do not recognize, click **Sign out everywhere else** and change your password.

## Workspace

The workspace owner can rename the workspace and change its picture under **Settings > General**. **Delete workspace** removes it for good.
