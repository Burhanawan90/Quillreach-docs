---
title: "Invite your team"
description: "Add teammates and choose what each one can see and do."
---

<Note>Only the workspace owner can manage members.</Note>

1. Go to **Settings > Members** and click **Invite teammate**.
2. Enter their email and click **Next**.
3. For each area, choose **No access**, **Limited access** or **Full access**. Use **Set all permissions** to set them all at once.
4. Click **Invite**.

Your teammate gets an email to join. From a member's row menu you can **Resend invite**, **Revoke invite**, **Edit permissions** or **Remove member**.
